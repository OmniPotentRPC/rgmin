- Added opt-in variable-cell relaxation. `Solver::set_cell_filter` puts a session on the extended `[positions, cell strain]` layout of ASE's `FrechetCellFilter` (matrix-log deformation, `exp_cell_factor` defaulting to N, scalar pressure, mask, hydrostatic and constant-volume options), configured through the `#[non_exhaustive]` `CellFilterConfig`; `Solver::step_cell` takes energy, forces and stress. The exponential preconditioner gains the `mu_c` cell block of Packwood et al., floored at 1 as ASE's `SparsePrecon` does. The C ABI minor is 48: `rgmin_cell_t`, `rgmin_cell_default`, `rgmin_solver_set_cell_filter`, `rgmin_solver_clear_cell_filter`, `rgmin_solver_step_cell`, `rgmin_solver_exp_mu_c` and `rgmin_solver_set_exp_mu_c`. Gold tests replay ASE 3.29.0 step by step on bulk Si and Cu.
