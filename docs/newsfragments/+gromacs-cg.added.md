Added GROMACS `integrator = cg` energy minimization as `Method::GromacsCg` with `GromacsCgConfig` and `GromacsCg`, the C selector `RGMIN_GROMACS_CG` with `rgmin_solver_set_gromacs_cg`, C++ `rgmin::Method::GromacsCg` with `Solver::set_gromacs_cg`, and Python `gromacs_mdp_options(integrator="cg")` with `rgmin.optimize.GromacsCg`. It reuses the Polak-Ribière NLCG core and strong-Wolfe line search, follows the `do_cg` steepest-descent restart phase for `nstcgsteep` (zero turns the periodic restart off, and `Solver::forget` starts the count again), caps `|beta|` at 5, stops on the largest per-atom force, and refuses holonomic constraints.
