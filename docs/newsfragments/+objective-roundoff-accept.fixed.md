`Solver::set_objective_roundoff` and `rgmin_solver_set_objective_roundoff` apply under every acceptance policy. The window used to hold only under `Accept::None`, so a C session, which opens with `RGMIN_ACCEPT_ENERGY`, dropped back to the four-epsilon window in its strong Wolfe search. A rise inside the configured window, such as `2^-24` at a value of 100 with a window of `1e-6`, was refused, and the step stayed at its start after the whole line-search budget. It is accepted again with two evaluations, as in 0.5. Manifold searches keep their own acceptance, and the `Accept::Energy` backtracking of a step that is not line-searched keeps its absolute `1e-8` rise. The C signature and ABI minor 43 are unchanged.
