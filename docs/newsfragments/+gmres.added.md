Added matrix-free GMRES with right preconditioning: `gmres_right` for one right-hand side and `gmres_right_block` for several independent ones whose Arnoldi directions share one batched operator call per iteration. Operators and preconditioners are fallible, every iteration streams to a `KrylovObserver`, and every reported residual is the true `‖b - A x‖` in the original system rather than the Givens estimate.
Refusals are typed (`KrylovRefusal`) and carry the full `KrylovRecord` collected before them, including the residual of the unchanged iterate when the Arnoldi step produces a zero subdiagonal. A non-finite value anywhere in the solve, including a NaN residual, is refused as `NonFinite`, and the block solver refuses exactly what the scalar solver refuses.
The error types implement `Display` and `std::error::Error`, and `GmresOptions`, `KrylovIteration`, `KrylovRecord` and `GmresRun` are non-exhaustive with `Default` and `with_*` setters.
