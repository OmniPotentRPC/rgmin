- Added Riemannian conjugate gradients as `minimize_rcg` and `Method::Rcg` (token `rcg`). The retraction, tangent projection and vector transport are the ones the manifold, the level-set hook and the holonomic bond set already provide. The coefficient is Fletcher-Reeves, PRP+ or Hager-Zhang. The transport is scaled when it lengthens a vector, or the identity in the Euclidean case. A direction that loses descent is replaced by the negative Riemannian gradient. The C ABI adds `rgmin_rcg_options_default` and `rgmin_minimize_rcg` at minor 53, and the Python session accepts `RiemannianCG`. In a session the method honours `maxmove` and the per-atom cap, composes a distance constraint set with the configured manifold, stops and reports on the gradient cut by the bonds, and clears its direction when a level-set retraction moves the point. `RcgOptions::validate` refuses a non-finite or negative tolerance and a non-finite or non-positive initial step with `Error::InvalidControl`, and a projection or retraction that fails is returned as a typed error.
