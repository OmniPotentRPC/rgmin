- Added Riemannian conjugate gradients as `minimize_rcg` and `Method::Rcg` (token `rcg`). The retraction, tangent projection and vector transport are the ones the manifold, the level-set hook and the holonomic bond set already provide. The coefficient is Fletcher-Reeves, PRP+ or Hager-Zhang. The transport is scaled when it lengthens a vector, or the identity in the Euclidean case. A direction that loses descent is replaced by the negative Riemannian gradient. The C ABI adds `rgmin_rcg_options_default` and `rgmin_minimize_rcg` at minor 51, and the Python session accepts `RiemannianCG`.
