Added Riemannian conjugate gradients as `minimize_rcg` and `Method::Rcg` (token `rcg`). The retraction, tangent projection and vector transport are the ones the manifold, the level-set hook and the holonomic bond set already provide. The coefficient is Fletcher-Reeves, PRP+ or Hager-Zhang. The transport is scaled when it lengthens a vector, or the identity in the Euclidean case. A direction that loses descent is replaced by the negative Riemannian gradient. In a session the method honours `maxmove` and the per-atom cap, composes a distance constraint set with the configured manifold, steps and stops on the field the session reports, including rigid quotients, products with rigid blocks and periodic sessions, keeps every trial in the objective's box, and clears its direction when a level-set retraction moves the point. A step that stalls on the energy noise floor keeps its start and reports `NoiseFloor`. `RcgOptions::validate` refuses a non-finite or negative tolerance and a non-finite or non-positive initial step with `Error::InvalidControl`, and a projection or retraction that fails, or a retraction slope that cannot be resolved, is returned as a typed error. The Python session accepts `rcg`, and `rgmin.optimize.RiemannianCG` runs it from ASE with the default per-atom cap off unless `maxstep` is set.
