Add Powell symmetric Broyden, SR1, and Bofill updates of a dense symmetric Hessian. The routines take row-major slices, reject non-finite input and mismatched lengths, and do not compare a step with an absolute size. The SR1 skip threshold is a finite value in [0, 1) and is accepted only by its constructor. Rank-one weights divide one square at a time, so an extreme step length keeps the secant residual at roundoff.
