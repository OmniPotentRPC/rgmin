Powell symmetric Broyden, SR1, and Bofill updates normalise the step and the residual and scale each outer product once by `|r|/|s|`. A step near `1e100` with a model near `1e-180`, and a step near `1e-80` with a model near `1e160`, meet the secant condition. The step is rescaled by a power of two before the model product is formed, and `|r|/|s|` is evaluated from the exponents of the two peaks, so steps and gradient changes with components anywhere from `1e-300` to `1e300` are accepted whenever the correction fits in an `f64`. A pair that cannot produce a finite correction returns an error or a skip and leaves the model unchanged.
