SCG with no iteration budget (`maxiter = 0`) stops on a flat plateau. After `max(n, 2)` consecutive accepted steps whose objective change passes `tol_func` and whose gradient norm never falls below its value at the first of them, it returns `Status::StepTolerance` (C `RGMIN_STOP_STEP_TOLERANCE`), or `Tolerance` or `NoiseFloor` when the gradient qualifies for those. Before, such a fit ran until `lambda_limit` and reported a gradient error, and it never returned when `lambda_limit` was at or above the internal damping cap of `1e100`. A single flat step, such as a Newton overshoot to a point of equal value, does not stop the fit. Fits with a positive `maxiter` follow the same trajectory as before. The C signature and ABI minor 43 are unchanged.
