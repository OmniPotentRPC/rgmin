- `Report` now carries a `StopReason` (`Converged`, `MaxIter`, `LineSearchFailed`, `NoiseFloor`, `StepTolerance`, `PolishUnmet`, `Iterating`) and `converged()`. A spent budget stays `Ok` with `Status::Budget`. `Control::with_error_on_unmet` returns `Error::StopUnmet` with the report. The gradient is tested after the last step as well as at the top of the loop.
- An `Lbfgs` or `Solver` session keeps its correction pairs, so `resume_objective` or another `step` continues from the returned point. A fresh solver does not.
- `Control::with_backward_polish` takes a shifted Newton or RFO step on a supplied Hessian or Hessian-vector product and accepts it only when the Newton decrement and every coordinate step sit under `backward_error_floor`. The floor is `n * f64::EPSILON * (1 + |V| + sum |x_i|)`.
- C ABI minor 48 adds `RGMIN_STOP_LINE_SEARCH`, `RGMIN_STOP_POLISH_UNMET`, `RGMIN_UNMET_STOP`, `rgmin_solver_set_error_on_unmet`, `rgmin_solver_set_backward_polish`, `rgmin_backward_error_floor`, and the `rgmin_minimize*_opts` entries. `rgmin_control_t` is unchanged. Python `SolverConfig` accepts the same two flags, `Report.converged` follows `Stop.TOLERANCE`, and `backward_error_floor` is the floor helper.
