Added `rgmin::aaa`, the AAA rational fit of Nakatsukasa, Sète and Trefethen (SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 40 (2018) A1494–A1522), with `aaa`, `AaaParams`, `Barycentric`, `Pole` and `chebyshev_pade_poles`, and `Error::Aaa` for its refusals.
`AaaParams` defaults to the paper's relative tolerance `1e-13` and 100 support points, and the support never takes every sample. The fit runs on samples mapped to `[-1, 1]` and values scaled by a power of two, so it is the same for data at `1e-200` and `1e200`. The weights come from a Householder `R` factor and one-sided Jacobi with an `n EPSILON` relative stop and a sweep budget that is refused when exceeded.
Numerical Froissart doublets, poles with a residue below `cleanup_tol · max|f|` times the sample half-width, are removed with their nearest support point before the weights are solved again. A zero weight is removed the same way, so every returned weight is nonzero. `Barycentric::poles` returns every pole and `Barycentric::poles_near_interval` the real and complex poles within one interval width of the samples, with residues.
