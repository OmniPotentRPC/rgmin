`ManifoldKind::SphereComplex` (`spherecomplex`, `RGMIN_MANIFOLD_SPHERE_COMPLEX` = 20): manopt `spherecomplexfactory`, the complex unit sphere.
Interleaved `(re, im)` pairs of length `2 n`.
Projection is `v - Re(x^* v) x`, retraction is `(x+v)/||x+v||`, transport is projection at arrival.
Not the real sphere and not `(S^1)^n`.
Tokens 7-10 stay reserved.
`abi_minor` 19.
