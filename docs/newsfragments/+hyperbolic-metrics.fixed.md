`Manifold::inner` and `Manifold::norm` on the Poincare ball are the conformal metric \(\lambda_x^2\, u^\top v\) with \(\lambda_x = 2 / (1 - \|x\|^2)\), and on the hyperboloid the Minkowski form, instead of the Euclidean product. These are the metrics in which each manifold's Riemannian gradient is defined, so the Riemannian CG norms, transport scaling and conjugacy coefficients, and the Riemannian L-BFGS and trust-region products now measure tangent vectors on both manifolds in the right metric.
