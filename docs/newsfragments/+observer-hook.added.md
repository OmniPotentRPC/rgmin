Added a general per-iteration observer hook across rgmin one-shot minimizers.

- Rust: `minimize_method_observed` plus observer-aware paths for L-BFGS, BFGS, steepest descent, nonlinear CG, SR1/SR2, Adam, particle swarm, SCG, and session-driven methods, with `minimize_adam_observed`, `minimize_pso_observed`, `minimize_scg_with_options_observed`, `minimize_scg_exact_with_options_observed` and `Lbfgs::minimize_objective_observed`. The observer sees each accepted iterate, the one that ends the run included, and receives iteration index, coordinates, value, gradient norm, and step length. It may stop cleanly with the distinct `ObserverRequested` stop reason, which `require_convergence` does not turn into `NotConverged`. `minimize_method_hess`, `minimize_method_hvp` and `minimize_rlbfgs` take no observer.
- C ABI: `rgmin_control_t` now carries optional `observer` and `observer_user` fields (ABI minor 59, layout revision 7), with `RGMIN_STOP_OBSERVER`, its `XTS_STOP_OBSERVER` alias, and observer action enums in `rgmin.h`. `rgmin_minimize`, `rgmin_minimize_opts`, `rgmin_minimize_eindir`, `rgmin_minimize_eindir_opts`, `rgmin_minimize_scg`, `rgmin_minimize_scg_with_options` and their `_sized` entries call it; the Hessian, Hessian-vector, Riemannian L-BFGS and `rgmin_solver_create` entries ignore it.
- Python: `rgmin.minimize(..., callback=...)` now accepts a scipy-style intermediate-result callback (`x`, `fun`, `nit`, `grad_norm`, `step_length`); raising `StopIteration` stops with `Stop.OBSERVER`, which `require_convergence=True` does not raise on.
