Added a general per-iteration observer hook across the minimizers.

- Rust: `IterationObserver` on the one-shot and session methods, Newton and dogleg, Newton-CG and Newton-PCG, Riemannian L-BFGS, Riemannian trust region, and the eOn conjugate-gradient step. The view is a flat coordinate vector plus the value, a stationarity scalar, and the Euclidean length of the accepted step. An unset observer does not allocate and is not called. A stop request uses `ObserverRequested`, which `require_convergence` does not turn into `NotConverged`.
- C ABI: `rgmin_control_t` carries optional `observer` and `observer_user` fields (ABI minor 68, layout revision 7), with `RGMIN_STOP_OBSERVER` and its `XTS_STOP_OBSERVER` alias. The one-shot, Hessian, Hessian-vector, eindir, SCG and Riemannian L-BFGS entries call it. `rgmin_solver_create` does not; session steps stay caller-driven.
- Python: `rgmin.minimize(..., callback=...)` accepts a callback with `x`, `fun`, `nit`, `grad_norm` and `step_length`. Raising `StopIteration` stops with `Stop.OBSERVER`, which `require_convergence=True` does not raise on.
