Riemannian CG measures the slope along its retraction with a step proportional to the trial length `t`. The old step was a fixed `sqrt(eps)`, which vanished next to `t` once the gradient norm fell below about `4e-9` and made the strong Wolfe curvature test accept every Armijo point, and which overshot `t` when the gradient norm passed about `7e7` and stalled the search on a cost of large scale.
