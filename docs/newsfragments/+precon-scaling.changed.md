`Exp` stores the Packwood et al. (2016) isotropic preconditioner `P = A ⊗ I_3` once as an `N × N` scalar CSR graph instead of a `3×` scalar-expanded `3N × 3N` CSR, cutting stored nonzeros by `3×` (`stored_nonzeros`, `csr_bytes`) and matrix-vector bandwidth by roughly `3×`.
Preconditioner PCG solves now precondition `Exp` with zero-fill incomplete Cholesky (`ExpPcKind::BlockIc0`, default; `ExpPcKind::Jacobi` opt-in) on 1,024-atom diagonal blocks and `Lindh`/`Ff` with exact `3 × 3` symmetric per-atom blocks, reuse workspace buffers with Rayleigh-projected warm starts (`with_warm_start`), stop at a configurable tolerance relative to the force norm (`DEFAULT_PRECON_RTOL = 1e-8`, `with_rtol`), and reduce dot products (`precon::dot`) without heap allocations.
`ExpParams`, `LindhParams`, `FfParams` (`with_threads`), `Solver` (`set_threads`, `threads`), and the batch helpers `Exp::assemble_batch` and `Exp::solve_batch` thread preconditioner assembly and PCG deterministically while keeping `threads = 1` serial by default.
