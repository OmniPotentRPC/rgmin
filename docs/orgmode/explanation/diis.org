#+TITLE: Pulay residual subspace
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

The step stores positions and gradients, oldest first, and drops
pairs past ~memory~. The coefficients ~c~ solve

: min || sum_i c_i g_i ||    subject to sum_i c_i = 1

which is the bordered Gram system of Pulay, Chem. Phys. Lett. 73,
393 (1980). Wood and Zunger, J. Phys. A, 1343 (1985), name the
residual form. No DOI is attached for that line.

One stored pair, or a Gram matrix with no usable pivot, moves by
~-istep * g~. Two or more pairs move to ~sum_i c_i x_i~. Neither
branch line searches on the oracle value, and neither applies a
kinetic preconditioner. ~Control::maxmove~ or
~Solver::set_atom_maxmove~ caps the displacement the same way the
other direct steps do.

~Method::diis~ keeps 6 pairs. The C selector is ~RGMIN_DIIS~ (22).
A control ~memory~ of 0 also means 6. ABI minor 30.
