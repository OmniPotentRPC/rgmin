* Choosing a preset

~Method::lbfgs~ keeps 10 pairs. That is the default. ~Method::lbfgs_wide~
keeps 20 and is not installed unless the caller asks for it.
~Solver::low_call_cg~ is nonlinear CG with the Dai-Yuan hybrid, the
Powell restart, and the case-3 average extrapolation. Default CG stays
Polak-Ribiere+ with the NJWS restart.

Pick from the cost of one force call.

- Under a few microseconds, use 10-pair L-BFGS. On the fifty LJ starts,
  20 pairs is faster only once a call costs more than 2.1 μs (LJ13),
  4.7 μs (LJ75), or 6.7 μs (LJ38). Below that, the extra two-loop costs
  more than the calls it saves.
- ~lbfgs_wide~ is a call-count option, not a speed preset. On the
  864-atom metal packings it loses wall time: 1.016× on EAM aluminium
  and 1.114× on EMT copper. Pt7 uses the same number of calls either way.
- Use ~low_call_cg~ instead of default CG on the LJ clusters at any
  positive call cost, and on the metal packings below. On Pt7 the
  fifty-start totals cross at about 8 μs. It is slower than L-BFGS on
  the cheap LJ runs.

A call of a few milliseconds makes the solver overhead negligible, so
equal call counts tie. The EAM aluminium slab is about 2.1 ms/call.
Seed 0 takes 15 L-BFGS calls with either memory and 27 CG calls with
either preset. The interleaved ratios are 1.001 [1.000, 1.002] and
1.003 [0.996, 1.010]. The EMT copper vacancy is about 2.2 ms/call, with
16 and 35 calls. The ratios are 1.002 [1.001, 1.002] and 1.004
[0.997, 1.014].

The LJ75 seed-0 run that uses more calls with 20 pairs is a different
basin, not a longer path to the same minimum. Its energy is 1.76 higher
(relative 4.6e-3). Over the fifty starts, 43 of 50 energies differ by
more than 1e-4 relative, the median absolute gap is about 1.2, and
neither run hits the known minimum.

Fifty random packings of a 6×6×6 cell, 864 atoms, take at least 200
calls. The EAM aluminium cell is at 4.038 Å with no pair closer than
2.15 Å. The EMT copper cell is at 3.595 Å with no pair closer than
1.90 Å.

~low_call_cg~ wins on both metals. The median call count falls from
1160.5 to 652.5 on aluminium (49 of 50 starts) and from 1262.5 to
937.0 on copper (42 of 50). The sum of those medians falls from
2423.0 to 1589.5. No median rises, the Mann-Whitney test does not find
higher energies, and every start still converges. On the start whose
call ratio is the median, the interleaved wall-time ratio against
default CG is 0.595321 [0.594953, 0.595723] on aluminium and 0.781444
[0.780747, 0.782044] on copper. The same-code bands are 1.000392
[0.999931, 1.000949] on aluminium and 0.999724 [0.998548, 1.000816]
on copper.

~lbfgs_wide~ does not win on wall time. The aluminium median rises
from 403.5 to 413.5, so the 50-start rule does not keep 20 pairs. The
copper median falls from 426.0 to 414.5. The wall-time ratios are
1.015799 [1.014578, 1.016935] on aluminium, against a same-code band
1.000349 [0.999861, 1.000829], and 1.113912 [1.108215, 1.119540] on
copper, against 1.000237 [0.999510, 1.000942]. Count calls with 20
pairs when that count is the quantity of interest. The speed default
stays ~Method::lbfgs~.
