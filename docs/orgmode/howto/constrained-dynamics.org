* Run constrained Langevin on a manifold

~baoab~ asks the caller for forces and uses the manifold for the drift, the projection, and the vector transport.
The same function covers a sphere, a product, and the flat hyperplane.
@@rst::doc:`../explanation/constrained-dynamics`@@ cites the schemes and the invariant.
@@rst::doc:`../reference/constrained-dynamics`@@ lists the types.

** A sphere

The height of a free particle on the unit sphere is uniform, and the mean tangent kinetic energy is \(kT\) for the two tangent directions.

#+begin_src rust
use ndarray::array;
use rgmin::dynamics::{LangevinConfig, PhasePoint, baoab};
use rgmin::manifold::{MassMetric, Sphere};

let metric = MassMetric::uniform(3, 1.0)?;
let mut phase = PhasePoint::default()
    .with_position(array![0.0, 0.0, 1.0])
    .with_velocity(array![0.2, 0.0, 0.0]);
let config = LangevinConfig::default()
    .with_thermal(0.8)
    .with_friction(1.0)
    .with_time_step(0.05)
    .with_seed(11);
let report = baoab(&Sphere, &metric, &mut phase, &config, &[], 100, |x| {
    Ok::<_, &'static str>((vec![0.0; x.len()], 0.0))
})?;
assert_eq!(report.degrees_of_freedom, 2);
#+end_src

** A product

A product of manifolds is a manifold.
Unit weights keep each factor in its own coordinates, and the normals are the factor normals placed in their blocks.

#+begin_src rust
use rgmin::manifold::{ManifoldKind, ProductFactor, ProductManifold};

let product = ProductManifold::new([
    ProductFactor::new(ManifoldKind::Sphere, 2),
    ProductFactor::new(ManifoldKind::Sphere, 2),
])?;
#+end_src

Pass ~&product~ to ~baoab~ with a mass metric of length four.

** The flat hyperplane

An affine hyperplane is the RATTLE special case: the drift is straight and the velocity projection is the inverse-mass product.
A specular wall is a ~PlanarWall~ on the allowed side \(n^\top(x - p) \le 0\).

#+begin_src rust
use ndarray::array;
use rgmin::dynamics::PlanarWall;
use rgmin::manifold::AffineHyperplane;

let plane = AffineHyperplane::new(array![0.0, 0.0, 0.0], array![0.0, 0.0, 1.0])?;
let wall = PlanarWall::new(array![0.0, 1.0, 0.0], array![0.0, 1.0, 0.0])?;
let _ = (plane, wall);
#+end_src

** Constrained velocity Verlet

~rattle~ is the same drift and kick without the thermostat.
On a Euclidean manifold it is unconstrained velocity Verlet.
The energy excursion of a smooth potential on the sphere scales as the square of the time step.
